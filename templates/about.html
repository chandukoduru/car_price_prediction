ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making resale estimates easier to understand.

AutoValue is an academic demonstration of a complete machine-learning workflow—from data preparation and model evaluation to a responsive web application.

Project overview

The application predicts a used car’s selling price from its manufacturing year, present price, kilometers driven, fuel, seller type, transmission, and previous owners. The target is learned from the CarDekho used-car dataset; the project compares Linear Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest regressors using a held-out test set.

Responsible interpretation

A prediction reflects patterns in the training data, not a physical inspection or a live market quote. Actual resale prices can vary by location, condition, service history, negotiation, and market timing. Always use the estimate as a reference—not a promise.
